Monte Cristo Sandwich

A sweet and savory fried ham, turkey, and Swiss cheese sandwich best served with a side of berry jam.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 tsp mayonnaise
  • 2 slices of cooked ham
  • 2 slices of cooked turkey meat
  • 1 slice of Swiss cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup milk

Steps

  1. Spread mayonnaise on one side of one bread slice. Spread mustard on one side of remaining bread slice.
  2. Top with alternate slices of ham, turkey, and Swiss cheese.
  3. Close sandwich with remaining bread slice, mayonnaise-side down.
  4. Beat egg and milk in a shallow bowl until well combined. Lightly grease a small skillet over medium heat.
  5. Dip sandwich into egg mixture to coat on both sides.
  6. Transfer sandwich to the hot skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides and cheese is melted.
  7. Serve while it's hot.
